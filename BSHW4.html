1: What sizes and fit options are available for dresses?
Our dresses are available in sizes XS–XXL with regular, petite, and plus-fit options. Please check the size chart on each product page for exact bust, waist, and hip measurements. If you're between sizes, consider sizing up for a relaxed fit. We offer free exchanges within 30 days if the fit isn't right.
2: What are the ingredients in your perfumes?
Our perfumes contain high-quality essential oils, natural extracts, and safe synthetic fragrances. Key ingredients include bergamot, jasmine, rose, sandalwood, and musk. All ingredients are listed on the product packaging and comply with international safety standards.
3: Are your makeup products suitable for sensitive skin?
Yes, all our makeup products are dermatologically tested and suitable for sensitive skin. We use non-comedogenic ingredients and avoid common irritants. However, we recommend doing a patch test before first use. If you have specific skin concerns, please consult our ingredient lists or your dermatologist.
